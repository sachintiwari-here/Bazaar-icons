Bazaar: Nearby Shops

Hyperlocal Shop Discovery, Price Comparison & Direct Ordering Platform

FounderSachin Kumar Tiwari
Document DateJune 4, 2026
Versionv1.0 — Original
StatusActive Development
01

Problem Statement

"Customers waste time visiting shops only to find items are out of stock or prices are higher than expected. Local shopkeepers lose customers to online platforms despite having the same products."

3–4x
Wasted trips to shops daily
63%
Prefer local shops if convenient
₹2L Cr
Quick commerce market by 2028

Core pain points identified:

  • ❌
    No price visibilityCustomers don't know prices before visiting — wasted trips
  • ❌
    No stock visibilityNo way to know if an item is available without physically going
  • ❌
    No comparison toolCan't compare prices across nearby shops easily
  • ❌
    Shopkeeper losing customersLocal shops losing business to Amazon/Flipkart despite proximity
02

The Solution

"A map-based mobile app where nearby shops are tagged with their live price lists, stock status, and customers can place pickup orders directly."

How it works — 3 steps:

🗺️
Discover
Find nearby shops on map with category filters
📋
Compare
View live price lists and compare across shops
📦
Reserve
Place pickup order — shop confirms, customer collects
03

Unique Value Proposition

"Bazaar is not a delivery app — it's a discovery + reservation platform for your actual local neighbourhood shops. No dark stores, no strangers — your familiar dukaan, digitized."

Key differentiators:

  • ✅
    Real local shops — not dark storesBlinkit/Zepto use warehouses. Bazaar uses actual neighbourhood shops customers already trust.
  • ✅
    QR Code viral loopShopkeepers share QR code with existing customers — organic, zero cost growth.
  • ✅
    Price comparisonSee same item's price across multiple nearby shops — unique in local commerce.
  • ✅
    Pickup-first modelNo delivery logistics — zero operational cost, instant scalability.
  • ✅
    Tier-2/3 city focusWhere Blinkit doesn't exist — 56% of India's e-commerce users are here.

09

Key Risks & Mitigations

🔴 High Risk

Chicken-Egg Problem

No customers without shops, no shops without customers

→ Start with shopkeeper side. 10 shops first, then invite customers.
🔴 High Risk

Stale Price Data

Shopkeeper forgets to update — customer gets wrong price

→ WhatsApp bot for quick updates. Penalty for wrong prices.
🔴 High Risk

Shopkeeper Resistance

Tech-averse, busy, "kaam chal raha hai" mindset

→ Founder manually sets up first 10. Show immediate value.
🟡 Medium Risk

Big Player Entry

Blinkit/Zepto expanding to Tier-2 cities

→ Real shop relationships = moat they can't replicate fast.
🟡 Medium Risk

Idea Theft

Partner/investor steals concept without compensation

→ NDA before any partnership. Trademark app name. This document.
🟡 Medium Risk

Solo Execution

One founder = slow speed, single point of failure

→ Find co-founder. Split tech + sales roles.
